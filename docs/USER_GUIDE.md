# C.A.R.B.O.N+ User Manual

Welcome to **C.A.R.B.O.N+**! This guide helps you navigate and make the most out of our carbon footprint platform.

## 🌟 Key Navigation Tabs

### 1. Dashboard
- **Eco Score**: Located on the right-hand panel, this gauges your overall sustainability on a scale of 0 to 100. Lower emissions and completed goals increase your score.
- **Footprint Summary**: Overview of your calculated Carbon footprints by Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly intervals.
- **Trend Charts**: Visualizes how your emissions stack up against carbon savings over the last 7 days.
- **Activity Log**: Lists recent activity entries with deletion capabilities.

### 2. Calculator
Log daily footprint metrics by completing the four key sections:
- **Transportation**: Select your commuting vehicle (e.g. Petrol Car, Bus, Train) and input your mileage in kilometers.
- **Electricity**: Enter your household energy usage in kWh and select the power source.
- **Dietary Preference**: Choose your dietary habits (Vegans produce ~50% less carbon than average mixed diets).
- **Waste**: Record your household waste weight in kg and select the disposal method.

### 3. Habit Tracker
Reward green deeds! Input quantities for actions:
- **Walking / Cycling**: Saves ~0.20 kg CO2e per km compared to single-passenger vehicle travel.
- **Public Transport**: Saves ~0.12 kg CO2e per km compared to driving.
- **Recycling**: Saves ~0.50 kg CO2e per recycled material container.
- **Energy Saving**: Saves ~0.82 kg CO2e per hour of turned-off appliances.

### 4. Goals Creator
Establish carbon reduction goals. Input a descriptive title, set the target reduction in kg, specify the end date, and click **Add**. Track your target progress and check off goals as you reduce your footprint!

### 5. Personalized Insights
Receive tailored suggestions. If commuting carbon is high, we will suggest eco-friendly transportation modes. If waste generation spikes, compost options are highlighted.

## 🌐 Regional Language Selection
To swap language interfaces, select from the language dropdown in the navbar. We support:
- English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Gujarati, Punjabi, Bengali, and Urdu.
Translations update instantly without reloading the page.
